# Jelly-filled Meltaways

## Ingredients

- 200 grams butter*
- 100 grams sugar
- One egg yolk
- 290 grams flour
- Preserves of choice
- Powdered sugar

## Instructions

1. Cream butter and sugar; add egg yolk and combine.
2. Mix in flour.
3. Roll dough into small balls and place on cookie sheet.
4. Indent center with the tip of your finger and fill with jelly or jam.
5. Bake in preheated oven at 170C (350F) for 10–12 minutes.
6. Cool and sprinkle with powdered sugar.

*The original recipe says 1/2 pound butter, 1/2 cup sugar and 2 cups sifted flour. I reduced the amount of butter by 10% because otherwise my cookies spread too much. People say it's because of the high fat content in French butter, so you may want to revert to the original measures if you are not making these in France.
