# Pizza Dough

## Ingredients

- All-purpose or bread flour: 250–295 g (2–2 1/3 cups)
- Instant yeast: 1 packet (2 1/4 tsp)
- Sugar: 1 1/2 tsp
- Salt: 3/4 tsp
- Optional garlic powder and/or dried basil: 1/8–1/4 tsp
- Warm water: 175 ml (3/4 cup), ideally 105–115°F
- Olive oil: 2 tbsp, plus extra for coating and brushing
- Toppings: quantity unspecified (see topping ideas below)

## Instructions

1. Mix 125 g flour with yeast, sugar, salt and optional seasonings. Stir in water and oil.
2. Incorporate another 125 g flour; add more as needed for a manageable, elastic, slightly tacky dough.
3. Form a ball, coat with oil in an oiled bowl, cover and let double, about 30 minutes. Heat oven to 425°F (215°C).
4. Deflate, knead 3–5 times, and stretch or roll into a 12-inch circle on a parchment-lined pizza pan. Shape the rim.
5. Brush with about 1 tbsp oil and prick the center with a fork. Add toppings; bake 13–15 minutes until golden.

Yield: one 10–12-inch pizza

Time: about 1 hour, including a 30-minute rise


### Topping ideas preserved from your original file

Tomato sauce, shallot, cherry tomatoes, mozzarella, and olive oil mixed with oregano, garlic and basil brushed over the dough.
