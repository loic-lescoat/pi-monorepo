# Bean and Cheese Tacos

## Ingredients

- 1/4 small white or yellow onion (about 1 oz), grated
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- One 15-oz can pinto beans, rinsed and drained
- 2 tsp sazón, adjusted to taste
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 4–8 tortillas, 5–6 inches across; flour preferred
- 6 oz grated cheddar or another melting cheese (about 1 1/2 cups)
- 3 tbsp coconut or vegetable oil
- Optional accompaniments: sour cream, salsa, hot sauce

## Instructions

1. Sauté onion and garlic in oil; mash in beans and sazón.
2. Add lime.
3. Warm tortillas, fill with cheese and hot beans, fold, and serve.
