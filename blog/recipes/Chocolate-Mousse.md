# Chocolate Mousse

## Ingredients

- 200 g chocolate tablet
- 5 eggs

## Instructions

1. Melt chocolate in a bain-marie.
2. Separate egg whites and yolks into two salad bowls.
3. Pour melted chocolate on egg yolks, mixing energetically.
4. Beat egg whites until stiff and add delicately in the preparation.
5. Pour mousse into 6 ramekins and place 4 hours in refrigerator.
