# Country Mushroom & Sour Cream Soup

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon of butter
- 1 large carrot, cut into chunks
- 2 large onions, sliced
- 1 pound fresh mushrooms (all "champignons de Paris" or substitute 20% cèpes), cut into halves
- 1 potato, cut into pieces
- 4 cups of water or stock
- 2 chicken bouillon cube (if not using stock)
- 1/2 cup cream
- Salt & paprika to taste

## Instructions

1. Melt butter in large soup pot.
2. Saute carrot and onion until limp, about ten minutes.
3. Add mushrooms and continue cooking another ten minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Add potato, water and bouillon cubes.
5. Bring to a boil, reduce heat and simmer 45 minutes.
6. Remove from heat and puree using an immersion blender.
7. Add cream, bring to a boil and simmer 5 minutes.
8. Add salt and paprika to taste. Enjoy!

Source: adapted from Twelve Months of Monastery Soups.

Note: The title refers to sour cream, but the ingredients and instructions specify cream. The source reads “Add salt a paprika”; transcribed as “Add salt and paprika.”
