# Gnocchi and veal-tomato sauce

## Ingredients

- Onion: 1 large
- Olive oil
- Tomato paste: ~250mL
- Veal: 500g, diced
- Tomato purée: 1L
- Potatoes: small armful
- Flour
- Water (quantity unspecified; mentioned only in instructions)

## Instructions

### Sauce

1. Slice up onion and cook in large pot with oil until translucent.
2. Add tomato paste and mix. Cook until darker red (3-4min).
3. Add veal and stir so it becomes seared on outside.
4. Add tomato purée. Cook 1h30-2h15.

### Gnocchi

1. Bake potatoes (unpeeled) in oven (no oil).
2. Use ricer to extract inside. If no ricer, cut potatoes in half and extract inside.
3. Knead potato innards to remove lumps.
4. Throw flour on clean table. Throw potato innards on top. Add flour1 and knead until homogenous (~feels like dry-ish play-doh).
5. Roll dough into “boudins” ~ 1 thumb across and 30-40cm long.
6. Cut into ~squares. Make thumbprint or fork print on each one.
7. Boil gnocchi in water until they float, then extract them.

You can freeze the gnocchi, no problem.
