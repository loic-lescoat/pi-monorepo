# Oatmeal Scotchies

## Ingredients

- 200 grams butter, softened (1 cup) (if using American butter: 220 grams)
- 150 grams dark brown sugar (original recipe said: 250 grams)
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract or grated peel of 1 orange
- 170 grams flour (1.25 cups)
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- If using unsalted butter, add ½ teaspoons salt.
- ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 250 grams quick or old-fashioned oats (3 cups Quaker oats “flocons d’avoine”)
- 200-300 grams butterscotch morsels or 200-300 grams seedless raisins

## Instructions

1. Blend butter and sugar(s).
2. Add eggs and vanilla to butter and sugar mixture and beat until uniform.
3. Add flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon; combine.
4. Add oats; mix.
5. Add butterscotch morsels or raisins.
6. Drop by rounded teaspoonful onto ungreased cookie sheet.
7. Bake in preheated 170C/350F oven for 8-9 minutes (chewy) or 9-10 minutes (crisp).
8. Enjoy!

Yield: About 4 dozen cookies.
