# fajitas

## Ingredients

- 4 peppers (ideally red, orange, yellow and green, but at least 2 colors), thinly sliced
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- A couple of teaspoons of olive oil
- Half a cup of water (optional, for really soft peppers)
- A spoonful of olive oil
- 450 grams (1 pound) chicken breast
- Salt (quantity unspecified)
- Pepper (quantity unspecified)
- Cumin (or “Mexican spice”; quantity unspecified)
- Juice of half a lemon (optional)
- Tortillas (quantity unspecified), served warm
- Guacamole (quantity unspecified): avocado, salt, lemon or lime juice, spices (quantities unspecified)
- Cream (quantity unspecified)
- Lettuce (quantity unspecified)
- Tomatoes (quantity unspecified)

## Instructions

1. Slice thin four peppers (ideally red, orange, yellow and green, but at least 2 colors).
2. Slice thin one onion.
3. Add a couple of teaspoons of olive oil to a big frying pan. Add the peppers and onions and cook until soft, stirring occasionally. It takes at least 20 minutes. If you like the peppers really soft, after 10 minutes add half a cup of water and cook until most of the water has evaporated, stirring occasionally.
4. Push the pepper-onion mixture to the side of the pan, add a spoonful of olive oil and then add 450 grams (1 pound) chicken breast. Cook few minutes, turning slices. When the chicken has turned mostly white (it doesn’t have to be fully cooked at this point), combine with peppers. Season with salt, pepper and cumin (or “Mexican spice”). Optional: add the juice of half a lemon. Cook until chicken is fully cooked through.
5. Serve with warm tortillas, guacamole (avocado, salt, lemon or lime juice, spices), cream, lettuce, tomatoes, etc. Enjoy!
