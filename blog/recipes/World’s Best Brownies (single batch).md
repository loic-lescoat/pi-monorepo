# World’s Best Brownies (single batch)

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup butter (2 ounces = 55 grams)
- 6 tablespoons Van Houten cocoa (45 grams)
- 1 cup granulated sugar (155 grams)
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup flour (75 grams)
- 1/8 teaspoon salt (omit if using salted butter)
- Walnuts (optional; quantity unspecified in source)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 305 F (150 C).
2. Melt butter at low heat in large saucepan.
3. Remove from heat and stir in cocoa.
4. Mix in granulated sugar.
5. Beat eggs with vanilla and add to mixture.
6. Stir in flour and salt (if using). Do not overmix.
7. Add walnuts (if using).
8. Spread in greased 8X8 inch (20X20 cm) baking pan (triple recipe: 13X9 inch).
9. Bake for about 25 minutes. Do not overbake.

## Tips

- Melt the butter in a saucepan that is large enough to be your mixing bowl, then you don’t have to pour it into another container.
- Van Houten cocoa is the best! or use another good quality Dutch cocoa.
- Make sure the butter/cocoa mixture has cooled a bit before adding eggs. Normally, once the sugar is mixed in, it is cool enough.
- Bake at only 305 F (150 C): le chocolat n’aime pas le chaud – never, ever go over 325 F (160 C). End baking when inserted knife comes out almost clean – may be less than twenty minutes depending on your oven.
- In my experience, kids do not like walnuts!
