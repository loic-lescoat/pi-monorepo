# Pancakes

## Ingredients

- 1 egg
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons cooking oil
- 1 cup flour = 150 gr.
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- Maple syrup (quantity unspecified in source)

## Instructions

1. Beat egg. Add milk and oil. Stir.
2. Combine flour, sugar, baking powder and salt. Add all at once to egg mixture. Stir until slightly lumpy.
3. Pour by spoonful onto hot, lightly greased griddle.
4. Turn over when its surface is bubbly and looks slightly dry around the edges.
5. Serve with maple syrup.
