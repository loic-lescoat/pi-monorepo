# Butterscotch brownies

## Ingredients

### Butterscotch brownies

- 50 grams butter
- 190 grams brown sugar*
- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 75 grams flour
- 1 teaspoon double-acting baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt (omit if using salted butter)

### Butterscotch brownies (double batch)

- 100 grams butter
- 380 grams brown sugar*
- 2 eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 150 grams flour
- 2 teaspoons double-acting baking powder
- 1 teaspoon salt (omit if using salted butter)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 170C (350F).
2. Melt butter in a saucepan big enough to be your mixing bowl.
3. Remove from heat and add brown sugar; mix well.
4. Beat egg with vanilla extract and add to butter and brown sugar mixture; stir until uniform.
5. Add flour, baking powder and salt; blend.
6. Pour into greased 8X8-inch pan and bake 20-25 minutes at 170C.
7. Cool and cut into squares.

Double batch: Use round 25-cm diameter pan and bake 25-30 minutes at 170C.

*Hygiena brand "Sucre de canne semoule foncé" is sold at Naturalia.
