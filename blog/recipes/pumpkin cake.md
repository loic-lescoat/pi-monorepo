# Paul’s Pumpkin Bars

## Ingredients

- 4 eggs
- 1⅔ cups granulated sugar
- 1 cup cooking oil
- 1 16-ounce can pumpkin
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking soda

### Cream Cheese Icing

- 1 3-ounce package cream cheese, softened
- ½ cup butter or margarine, softened
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 2 cups sifted powdered sugar

## Instructions

1. In mixer bowl, beat together eggs, granulated sugar, oil, and pumpkin till light and fluffy.
2. Stir together flour, baking powder, cinnamon, salt, and soda. Add to pumpkin mixture and mix thoroughly.
3. Spread batter in ungreased 15x10x1-inch baking pan. Bake in 350° oven for 25 to 30 minutes. Cool.
4. Frost with Cream Cheese Icing. Cut in bars.
5. To make Cream Cheese Icing: Cream together cream cheese and butter or margarine. Stir in vanilla. Add powdered sugar, a little at a time, beating well, till mixture is smooth.

Makes 2 dozen.
