# Pumpkin Soup

## Ingredients

- Two to four tablespoons butter
- Two large onions, sliced
- One kilo fresh pumpkin, peeled and cut into chunks, or two cans of Libby’s pureed pumpkin in a can
- One can beer (such as Heineken or 1664)
- Four cups water (more if needed)
- Two chicken bouillon cubes
- 180 grams cheddar cheese, grated (optional for Loïc[unclear: ()])

## Instructions

1. Melt butter in large soup pot, and saute onions for at least ten minutes until they are limp and translucent.
2. Add pumpkin, beer, water and bouillon cubes. Bring to a boil; reduce heat and simmer until pumpkin is soft and falls apart (about half an hour).
3. Remove from heat and puree with an immersion blender.
4. Add grated cheese and stir to melt. Enjoy!

Note: The cheese’s optional qualifier has malformed punctuation in the source: “(optional for Loïc()”.
