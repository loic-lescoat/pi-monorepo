# Chocolate Chip cookies – original Toll House recipe

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (2 sticks) butter, softened = 220 grams in US, 200 grams in France
- 250 grams dark brown sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour = 300 grams
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt (omit if using salted butter)
- 175 grams of chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350F/170C.
2. Cream together butter and sugar(s). Add eggs and vanilla extract and combine well. Add flour, baking soda and salt; mix until smooth. Add chocolate chips and mix.
3. Drop by rounded spoonful on to a cookie sheet and bake at 350F/170C for 8-10 minutes.
